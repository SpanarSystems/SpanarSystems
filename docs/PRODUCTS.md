# Spanar Systems product portfolio

## In development
- **Velocity** — AI-native multi-tenant SaaS operating platform.
- **Temple** — spiritual journey discovery and planning guide.
- **Travai** — AI travel companion and adaptive tour planner.

## Concepts
- **Relay** — AI workflow coordination and operational hand-offs.
- **Prism** — conversational operational intelligence.

## Product language rules
Do not present roadmap ideas as shipped features. Use **In development** for Velocity, Temple and Travai. Use **Concept** for Relay and Prism until formally promoted.

## Site structure
Product pages live in `/products`; shared presentation is in `/assets/css/spanar-next.css`; interaction code is in `/assets/js/spanar-next.js`.

## Solutions pages (SEO)
`/solutions/*` pages, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and the solutions/contact blocks in `index.html` are generated by `python3 tools/build_solutions.py`. Set `SALES_EMAIL` / `SALES_PHONE` in that script and re-run to update contact details site-wide. Sample screens are illustrative UI with dummy data, not shipped products.
