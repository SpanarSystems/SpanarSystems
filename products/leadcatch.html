FAQ

Common questions.

Does LeadCatch generate leads?

No. LeadCatch processes enquiries you already receive and moves their details into HubSpot.

Will it create duplicate contacts?

LeadCatch checks for an existing contact before creating a new record and can send uncertain matches for review.

Is LeadCatch available?

LeadCatch is in pilot. Contact Spanar Systems to test it with your IndiaMART and HubSpot workflow.
